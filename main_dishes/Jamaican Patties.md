## Ingredients

### Dough

* 3 cups flour
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1 Tablespoon Jamaican curry powder
* 2 teaspoons turmeric powder
* 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
* 3/4 cup cold water
* 1 Tablespoon white vinegar
* 1 large egg, lightly whisked

### Filling

* 1 pound ground beef
* 1 yellow onion, diced
* 1 Scotch bonnet pepper, stemmed, seeded, and minced
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 1/4 cups beef stock
* 1 1/2 Tablespoons steak sauce
* 1 1/2 Tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
* 1 teaspoon onion powder
* 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
* 1 Tablespoon Jamaican curry powder
* 1/2 teaspoon allspice
* 1 teaspoon kosher salt
* 2 bay leaves
* 4 sprigs thyme
* 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
* 1 egg, beaten, for egg wash

## Directions

For the dough, combine the flour, salt, curry powder, and turmeric in a large bowl. Using your fingers, rub the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs. Whisk together the water, vinegar, and egg, and stir into the flour. Form into a ball, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate at least 1 hour.

For the filling, season the beef generously with salt and pepper, and brown in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Reduce the heat to medium, and add the onion, pepper, and garlic. Cook, stirring often, until the vegetables are soft.

Add the beef stock, steak sauce, Worcestershire sauce, onion powder, cayenne, curry powder, allspice, salt, bay leaves, and thyme. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer until most of the liquid has evaporated, about 12 minutes. Remove from the heat, discard the bay leaves and thyme, and season to taste with salt and pepper. Stir in the breadcrumbs.

Heat the oven to 375°F. Divide the dough in half and roll out to 1/8-inch thickness on a lightly-floured work surface. Cut the dough into 6-inch circles and place 2 heaping Tablespoons of meat filling onto half of each round of dough. Brush the edges of dough with egg wash. Fold the dough over filling and press the edges with the tines of a fork to seal. Brush the tops lightly with the remaining egg wash.

Place the patties on a lined baking sheet, and bake 25 minutes. Allow to cool for 5 minutes before serving.
