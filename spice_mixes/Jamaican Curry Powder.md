## Ingredients

* 1 Tablespoon yellow mustard seeds
* 1 Tablespoon fenugreek seeds
* 1 Tablespoon whole allspice berries
* 1 1/2 teaspoons coriander seeds
* 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds
* 3/4 teaspoon black peppercorns
* 1 whole clove
* 1 star anise
* 1 dried red chile
* 4 teaspoons ground turmeric
* 1 Tablespoon ground ginger
* 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg

## Directions

Lightly toast the mustard, fenugreek, allspice, coriander, cumin, peppercorns, clove, anise, and chile in a dry skillet over low heat, 3 - 5 minutes.

Remove from the heat. Grind into a fine powder and combine with the turmeric, ginger, and nutmeg.
